## Images are not covered

No image is relicensed by this repository. Each manifest row carries the licence
asserted at its source (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, or all rights reserved). Only the
1,432 rows listed in `data/manifests/release_manifest.csv` may be redistributed;
the remaining 4,537 are metadata-only and must be re-fetched from the URL in the
manifest, subject to that record's own licence.
